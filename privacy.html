Information we collect

  • To send the newsletter and updates about books, chapters and videos in the Fae Academy series.
  • To reply to messages you send us.
  • To keep the Site secure and working.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

Who we share it with

We use service providers to run the Site and the newsletter: our email service provider (which stores subscriber emails and sends the newsletter) and our web hosting provider. They may process your information only to provide their services to us. We may also disclose information if the law requires it.

Legal basis (EEA and UK visitors)

We send the newsletter based on your consent, which you can withdraw at any time. We handle messages and server logs based on our legitimate interests in answering you and keeping the Site secure. Your information may be processed in the United States.

How long we keep it

Newsletter emails are kept until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete them. Messages are kept for as long as needed to respond and for a reasonable period afterward. Server logs are kept for the period set by our host.

Your choices and rights

  • Unsubscribe at any time with the link at the bottom of every email, or email us.
  • California residents may ask to know what personal information we hold about them, to correct it, or to delete it. We will not discriminate against you for making a request.

To make a request, email lyra@lyrablackwood.com. We may need to confirm your identity first.

The Site and newsletter are intended for visitors 16 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13, or from anyone under 16 without a parent's permission. If you believe a child has given us information, email us and we will delete it.

The Site may link to other websites, such as retailers or video platforms. We do not control them and are not responsible for their privacy practices.

Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information, but no website or email system is completely secure.

Changes to this policy
